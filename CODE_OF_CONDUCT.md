# Code of Conduct

Civic Signal Kit welcomes people who want to make public-interest data easier to understand.

## Expected Behavior

- Be respectful.
- Give specific, actionable feedback.
- Make room for beginners.
- Respect privacy and context around civic data.

## Unacceptable Behavior

- Harassment, threats, or personal attacks.
- Publishing private information without permission.
- Discriminatory language or conduct.
- Repeated disruption of project work.

Maintainers may remove comments, close issues, or block users when needed to protect the project.
